## Change these (stage 1)
1. **Category:**
   - primary: **<primary category>**;
   - additional, if the picker offers them under these exact names: <additional categories>. Skip any that do not exist; do not invent substitutes;
   - remove: <wrong category, if any>.

   `<description: 3–5 plain sentences, under 750 characters; what the business is, main services, area, how bookings/consultations work; no keywords list, no URL, no phone, no forbidden claims>`

7. **Review replies:** reply to every review that has no owner reply yet.
   - 1–2 polite, varied sentences.
   - No case or customer details, no promises or results, no keywords, no phone or URL.
   - Skip negative reviews and reviews that describe a specific matter; list them for me.

## Do NOT
- Change the business name, the address or the map pin. Address changes are stage 2, done alone, because they can trigger re-verification.
- Delete any review, including one written from this account. Report it; the owner removes it personally.
- Claim, edit or review any other listing.
- Write reviews, upload photos, create posts, change attributes, or accept terms, ads or paid offers.
- If Google asks for re-verification or shows an unexpected dialog: stop and ask me.

## Process
1. Before editing, screenshot each section: category, hours, description, services, phones.
2. Edit one section at a time and save each. Before saving, read the fields back to confirm they are exact.
3. Re-open the profile and confirm what Google now shows. Note "under review" or "Google updated your profile" notices.

## Report
- Table: Section | Before | After | Status (saved / under review / failed + exact message).
- The categories actually available in the picker.
- Review replies posted, failed (with the exact error) and skipped (with the reason).
- Attributes currently shown, for the owner to verify on site (flag anything that may be untrue).
- Every warning or verification request, quoted exactly.
